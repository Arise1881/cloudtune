CloudTune is a free and open-source music player focused on Turkish internet radio and online audio.

- Browse and play radio stations with station logos
- Search online music, open links and build playlists
- Sync playback with a friend in two-device rooms
- Room invite links with optional password protection
- Equalizer, shuffle, repeat, sleep timer and queue controls
- Background playback with lock-screen and notification controls
- Mobile data and Wi-Fi aware traffic optimization
- Library, history, playlists, accent colors and themes are preserved
- 28 interface languages

CloudTune uses public internet radio streams and online metadata services. An internet connection is required for radio, search and cover art.
